## Where to post
- LinkedIn: owners and finance managers of SMEs and NGOs; Africa diaspora business groups.

## Rules
- Send to people in a specific country, then tag responses by country. Never blend markets in the analysis.
- Target ≥20 responses per region before drawing any conclusion about that region.
- Send in their morning, local time zone.
