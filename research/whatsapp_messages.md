## Rules
- Send 1-to-1 messages first (aim: 30 people). Group posts get fewer replies.
- Best times: 7–9am or 12–2pm. Avoid late evening.
- Reply personally to everyone who answers. They are your first potential customers.
- Never pressure anyone to share theft stories. Keep it optional and anonymous.
